My Favorite City - Seoul of South Korea

History

Seoul is the capital of South Krea itself and it has a really long history that dates back to over 2,000 years ago. It became a really important city during the Joseon Dynasty because it was chosen as the capital of South Korea during that time. Which in turn the king, government officials, and other important people had lived there which made it really popular for politics, trade, education, and culture itself. This lasted from 1392 to 1910. Even as of today you can still see some stuff from the past in places that are traditional buildings or really old palaces.Seoul has continued to grow and is now a major city known for technology, entertainment, business, and Korean culture.
